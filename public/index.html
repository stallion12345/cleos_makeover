Your photo is sent through this app's server to YouCam's AI service for analysis, which keeps results for up to 24 hours. The server does not save it, and this app keeps your photo only if you choose to put it on your Skin ID, on your device only.

About

Your guide is Cleo, named for Cleopatra VII, the last active ruler of Ptolemaic Egypt. Artwork is abstract; no portraits of these women exist, and each profile is inspired by her, not a claim about her skin. This is skincare guidance, not medical advice.
